UK Residents

If you are located in the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right under the Data (Use and Access) Act to lodge a formal data protection complaint directly with us if you believe we have mishandled your personal data or breached your privacy rights.

To submit a data protection complaint, please email our dedicated team at MedscapePrivacy@webmd.net with the subject line "UK Data Issue".

We will formally acknowledge receipt of your complaint in writing within 30 days. We will then investigate the matter thoroughly and without undue delay, aiming to provide you with a final written outcome and resolution within three months.

If you are unsatisfied with our final response, you have the right to contact the UK's independent data regulatory body, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You can reach them via their website at www.ico.org.uk, by phone at 0303 123 1113, or by writing to: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.
